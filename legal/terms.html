1Who we are

Puddleton is a free pixel Mafia party game made by After Dark, an independent studio run by C. Siddarth Ram in Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at sidsvictus@gmail.com. How we handle data is explained in our privacy policy, and tips for staying safe are in the Safety Center.

2Playing as a guest

You play as a guest. Your device keeps a random guest id and secret that let the game recognise you. If you clear them, or lose your device, your progress is gone and we cannot bring it back. Don't share your guest data with anyone.

When you start, you tell us your age band: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over. Please be honest: it decides your chat and safety settings. Pretending to be older to get around them breaks these terms.

3Rules of play

Puddleton is a game of bluffing. Inside a match you may lie about your role, accuse others and keep secrets. That is the fun. What is not allowed:

  • Ghosting: sharing roles, night actions or chat from a match through anything outside the game, such as another app or a call with players at a different table.
  • Teaming up across sides or agreeing results before a match.
  • Playing more than one seat in the same game.
  • Cheating tools: bots, scripts, modified clients, or exploiting bugs (please tell us about bugs instead).
  • Throwing or quitting on purpose again and again to spoil games for others.
  • Interfering with the service: flooding, attacking or trying to get around the bot check, bans or limits.

4Code of conduct

These rules apply to chat, whispers, display names and anything else you put into the game.

  • Be kind outside the role. No bullying, harassment, insults about who someone is, or picking on a player across games.
  • No hate. Nothing that attacks people for their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
  • No threats or violence beyond the cartoon game itself, and nothing that encourages self-harm or suicide.
  • No sexual content, and never anything sexual involving children.
  • Keep personal information private. Don't share your own or anyone else's real name, address, school, phone number, social media or photos, and don't ask other players for theirs.
  • Don't ask players to move to another app, to keep secrets from their parents, or how old they are or where they live.
  • No spam, scams or advertising, and no pretending to be After Dark staff or another player.
  • Nothing illegal, and no links to anything that breaks these rules.

5How moderation works

We use a mix of tools and people. Here is exactly what happens.

Settings by age
Under 13s chat with ready-made phrases only and cannot whisper or add friends. Players 13 and over type through filters. Chat with strangers is for adults only and is off until they turn it on. Whispers are announced to the whole table (not what they say).
Filters
Automatic word filters check typed chat and names, and block or hide words and patterns we don't allow. Filters act on messages; they don't ban anyone on their own.
Mute and block
Any player can mute or block another player for themselves at any time. See the Safety Center for how.
Reports
Players can report someone from inside the game. A report includes a snapshot of that match's log (public chat, whispers, votes and actions) so we can see what actually happened.
Review by a person
Reports are reviewed by a person at After Dark. Sanctions are decided or checked by a person, never by a filter alone.
What we can do
Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before: a warning, resetting a display name, removing content, a chat mute, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. Bans apply to the guest id and the device, so starting over as a new guest does not get around them.
Statement of reasons
When we sanction you, we tell you what we did, which rule it was for, what content or behaviour it was about, whether any automatic tool was involved, how long it lasts, and how to appeal.

We do not read every chat. We look at match logs when something is reported, when we must investigate abuse, or when the law requires it.

6Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, appeal. Email sidsvictus@gmail.com with "Appeal" in the subject, your display name, roughly when it happened and why you think the decision was wrong. You can appeal for up to six months after the decision.

A person looks at the decision again, with the report and the match log, and we aim to reply within 14 days. If we were wrong, we lift the sanction and remove it from your record. If you live in the EU, you may also be able to take a dispute to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body.

7Reporting illegal content

Anyone, player or not, can tell us about content they believe is illegal: use Report in the game or email us. Say where it was (the room and roughly when), what it was, and why you think it is illegal, and include your name and email unless the report concerns child sexual abuse. We confirm we received it and tell you what we decided.

We report child sexual abuse material and attempts to exploit children to the authorities, including the US National Center for Missing and Exploited Children and any other authority where the law requires. If we think someone's life or safety is in danger, we tell the police.

So that the game works, you allow us to show them to the players in your match, to store them in match logs and reports for the times in our privacy policy, and to use them for moderation and to follow the law. We may remove anything that breaks these terms. You are responsible for what you write.

Items have no money value, cannot be sold, traded or cashed out, and we may change, rebalance or remove them as the game develops. Before we ever sell anything, we will update these terms with the details (prices, refunds and your rights as a consumer) and tell you in the game.

Puddleton is in beta. Features will change, things may break, and progress may occasionally be reset. When the server is at capacity, the island is full and new games may be refused until it frees up. We may update the game at any time, and we may change these terms; if a change matters we will tell you in the game before it takes effect, and the date at the top will change.
