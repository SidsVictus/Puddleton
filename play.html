            The Puddleton Fable

            Chapter 1Welcome to Puddleton

            Puddleton floats above a sea of clouds: jelly-bean folk, drifting lanterns, the smell of fresh muffins. But when the moon comes up, someone puts on a tiny fedora, and the Mafia goes to work.

            Every game is a fable with two halves that repeat until one side wins: Night, for secret moves, and Day, for talking and voting.

            Chapter 2Opening the door

            One friend taps Create a room and gets a six-letter code. Everyone else types it into Join. The creator picks who tells the story: the town clock, which runs everything while the creator plays, or a human Grandmaster, who narrates and does not play.

            The Grandmaster sees everything, every role and every whisper, just like a narrator should.

            Chapter 3When night falls

            At night, players with powers tap a face to use them. Everyone else sleeps. When the sun rises, the story reveals who did not wake up, and what role they had.

            By day, the whole town talks and votes. The player with the most votes is sent off the island. A tie means nobody goes. You can change your vote or abstain until the day ends.

            Chapter 4The Town wants every Mafia gone

            Villager
            No powers, just a big heart and a sharp vote.
            Angel
            Each night, shields one person, even themselves. If anyone tries to kill them, the Angel saves them before they die.
            Detective
            Each night, inspects one person and learns "mafia" or "innocent".
            Vigilante
            Each night, may take a shot at someone. Aim carefully.
            Mayor
            Their day vote counts twice.

            Chapter 5The Mafia wants to outnumber the town

            Mafia
            Each night, picks someone to eliminate. Mafia members know each other.
            Godfather
            The boss: their pick is final, and they look "innocent" to the Detective.
            Framer
            Does not kill; frames someone so the Detective sees them as "mafia" that night.

            The Mafia wins when they are at least as many as everyone else, once the Serial Killer is gone.

            Chapter 6The wildcards play for themselves

            Jester
            Wins alone if the town votes them out. Acts suspicious on purpose.
            Serial Killer
            Kills someone every night and wins by being the last one standing.

            Chapter 7Pick your mode

            Classic
            Villager, Angel, Detective, Mafia and Godfather. The timeless game.
            Crazy
            All 10 roles in play: framed alibis, surprise shots, a Jester and maybe a Serial Killer.
            Gore
            Classic roles, but the narration turns cartoon-gruesome.
            Speed
            Classic roles with lightning-fast timers. Whole games in minutes.

            Chapter 8Talking

            Whispers, the private messages, are always open: pick a player and write to them. Only the two of you, and the Grandmaster, can read it. The Town Square group chat opens during the day when the host switches it on. The dead tell no tales: once you are out, you can only watch.

            Find the Mafia, trust no muffin, and have fun. This manual is always available in the Help section.
